Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

Spendly is built around a simple promise: your financial data belongs to you. This page explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what we never do with it. We try to keep this short, honest, and free of legalese wherever possible.

1. Data We Collect

To provide the service, we collect only what we need to make Spendly work for you:

  • Account information — the email address and password you use to create your account. We use your email for sign-in and important service notifications.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions of the expenses you log. This is the core of what Spendly stores for you.
  • Session data — short-lived cookies or tokens that keep you signed in. These expire when you sign out or after a long period of inactivity.

We do not collect your bank account numbers, card details, or transaction data from your bank. Spendly works entirely on the information you choose to enter yourself.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data above only to operate Spendly. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Display your expenses, summaries, and category breakdowns.
  • Let you filter and review your spending by date or category.
  • Keep your account secure and notify you about important changes.
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve the service.

We do not use your expense data to train advertising models, we do not build a credit profile from it, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers with encryption in transit and at rest. Access to production data is limited to a small number of authorized personnel who need it to operate the service. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

While we work hard to protect your information, no system is perfectly secure. If we ever become aware of a breach that affects your data, we will notify you promptly by email and describe what happened and what we are doing about it.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You can delete any individual expense at any time from the expense list. If you decide to close your account, your expense records and account information are permanently removed from our active systems within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (for example, tax or anti-fraud records).

You can also request a copy of all data we hold about you by emailing us at the address below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

5. Third Party Services

To run Spendly, we rely on a small number of trusted third parties — for example, a hosting provider for our servers and an email service for transactional emails. These providers only receive the minimum information needed to perform their role and are contractually obligated to protect it.

We do not embed third-party advertising, analytics trackers, or social-media pixels in Spendly. The only analytics we may collect are aggregated, anonymous counts (such as the number of expenses logged in a day) used solely to understand usage and improve the service.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data we hold about you. You can do most of this from your account settings. For anything else — including a full export of your data or account closure — please reach out using the contact details below.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children's Privacy

Spendly is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will close it and delete the associated data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the service or our practices. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will notify you through the service or by email before the changes take effect. Continued use of Spendly after the effective date constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or if you would like to exercise any of your rights, please contact us at privacy@spendly.app. We read every message and will get back to you as quickly as we can.
